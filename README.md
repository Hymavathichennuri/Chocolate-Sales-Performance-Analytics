# Chocolate-Sales-Performance-Analytics
1.Project Title: Chocolate Sales Performance Analytics Dashboard

2.Overview
• A dynamic, interactive data visualization tool built to analyze Awesome Chocolates sales between 03/03/2024 – 31/03/2025.

3.Description
• The chocolate Sales Performance Analytics Dashboard is a visually engaging and analytical Power BI report designed to analyze chocolate sales, profit, shipment volume, and product performance across multiple regions.
• Created KPIs to track Total Sales (77.11M), Total Profit (44.23M), Profit Percentage (57.4%), Shipment Count (14K), and Total Boxes Sold (5M).
• Designed visualizations including trend analysis, geographic sales distribution, product performance, shipment distribution, and salesperson performance.
• Implemented date slicers, drill-down capabilities, and interactive filters to enable dynamic business analysis.

4.Tech Stack
The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:
• Power BI Desktop – Main data visualization platform used for report creation.
• Power Query – Data transformation and cleaning layer for reshaping and preparing the data.
• DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – Used for calculated measures, dynamic visuals, and conditional logic.
• Data Modeling – Relationships established among tables (Calendar, Location, People, Shipments, Products).
• File Format – .pbix for development and .png for dashboard preview.

5.Data Source
• From Kaggle.com websibte 

6.Features
• Interactive Date Range Filter (2024–2025) for trend analysis.
• KPI Cards displaying Total Sales Amount, Total Profit, Profit Percentage, Total Boxes Sold, and Shipment Count.
• Monthly Sales Trend Analysis to identify growth and seasonal patterns.
• Monthly Boxes Sold Trend to track product demand.
• Shipment Distribution Analysis to understand shipment frequency and volume.
• Top 6 Products Analysis based on sales performance.
• Regional Sales Distribution using Geo Analysis.
• Salesperson Performance Dashboard showing Revenue Generated, Boxes Sold, and Profit Contribution.
• Product-wise Profitability Analysis with conditional formatting.
• Interactive Filtering and Cross-Highlighting between visuals.

7.Business Highlights / Insights
• Generated 77.11M Total Revenue and 44.23M Total Profit with an overall 57.4% Profit Margin.
• Analyzed over 5 Million Boxes Sold across multiple products and regions.
• Monitored 14,000 Shipments to understand distribution patterns and operational performance.
• Identified top-performing products contributing the highest revenue and profit.
• Compared sales performance across different countries to identify key revenue-generating markets.
• Evaluated salesperson performance to recognize top contributors and improvement opportunities.
